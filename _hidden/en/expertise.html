01 — Services

What can be commissioned

Work on Arabic sources, from a single manuscript to the sources component of a European project. Every deliverable states what the evidence supports and where it stops.

01

Manuscript identification & dating

Identification of the text, the copyist, the date and the provenance of Arabic manuscripts; physical description; assessment of lacunae, later interventions and doubtful attributions.

DeliverableAn identification note: text, author, copyist, date, provenance, physical description, and the argument for the dating.

02

Critical edition & translation support

Establishing Arabic texts from their witnesses, or revising a team's translation against the original manuscript, flagging omissions, misreadings and uncertain passages.

DeliverableAn edited text with apparatus, or a revision memo keyed to the translation.

03

Source-based historical consultancy

Source dossiers for museums, exhibitions and documentaries: what the documents say, what they cannot support, and how to present the evidence without overstating it.

DeliverableA dossier of translated and commented sources, with a note on uncertainty.

04

Work-package support in EU projects

Drafting the Arabic-sources component of a work package, reviewing the methodology, and writing deliverable reports in English or French.

DeliverableWP text, methodology note and deliverable report.
