01 — Books & articles

Selected publications

Work on water and urban form, hydraulic technologies, toponymy and the history of the medieval Maghrib.

2023Book

Water and Urbanisation in the Medieval Maghrib: the South-East and the Qibla Lands

Monograph, Cadi Ayyad University Press, 2023. A study of the relationship between water resources and urban form, with attention to traditional hydraulic systems and their social and economic role.

With Abdellah Amjihadi and Nour Eddine Ait Ouchen. Kan Historical Journal, vol. 19, no. 80, pp. 140–155, 2026.
