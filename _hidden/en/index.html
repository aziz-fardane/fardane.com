01 — Profile

Who you would be working with

A historian of the medieval Maghrib, working from Arabic manuscripts, archival documents and toponymy. Based in Marrakesh, available to project teams in Europe and beyond.

I am Aziz Fardane, a historian of the medieval Maghrib based in Marrakesh. My work reconstructs the relationship between water and urban form through Arabic manuscripts, archival documents and toponymy.

I support research teams, museums and heritage projects that need a specialist able to read, date and interpret Arabic sources — and to say clearly what those sources can and cannot support.

Trained at Cadi Ayyad University, I work in Arabic, French and English, on site in Morocco or remotely with project teams across Europe.

02 — Evidence

Published, peer-reviewed, documented

Three verifiable references: a monograph, a peer-reviewed article with a DOI, and a technical specialism grounded in the Arabic texts.

Water and Urbanisation in the Medieval Maghrib

A study of the relationship between water resources and urban form in the south-east of Morocco and the qibla lands, based on manuscript and archival evidence.
